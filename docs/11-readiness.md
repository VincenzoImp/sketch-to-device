## Human involvement and acceptance

No new key, network credential, board identity or setup permission is needed. The user will supply the next real app request and test the resulting app. The assistant owns backup, publication, diagnosis, implementation and appropriate checks.

Earlier schema 1 voice Rocket Tata, photo-only Star Tap and text Hello Colors have physical behavior evidence. Programmable gameplay, combined photo+voice, interrupted installation, repeated requests and broader fidelity are separate gates; the observed reset/restoration alone does not close them. Camera, microphone and speaker remain unavailable to generated apps until native APIs and physical checks exist.

The original v3 Penguin backup is preserved, and the additional stabilized v8 snapshot contains its exact package/source, configured BIN/ELF, manifest and consistent database snapshot. Recovery must retain fresh deployment/version identity and validation; never overwrite the live database or clear quarantine to force success.
